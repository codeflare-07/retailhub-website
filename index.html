Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is RetailHub really free?

Yes, RetailHub is 100% free with no monthly fees, no licensing costs, and no hidden features. It runs as a local offline application on your Windows machine.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Absolutely! RetailHub is designed to be offline-first. Your inventory logs, billing registries, and receipts are stored locally on your PC. You only need the internet if you want to sync your data backups to Google Drive or check for updates.

How does the Google Drive auto-backup work?

In the application settings, you can authenticate your Google Account. RetailHub will then create a private, encrypted database folder in your personal Google Drive and automatically sync local data to the cloud when an internet connection is available, keeping your data safe from PC failures.

How do auto-updates work in v1.1.9?

Starting with version 1.1.9, RetailHub checks for updates automatically on startup. When a new stable version is available, it notifies you and lets you update instantly with a single click, keeping your security patches and features up-to-date.
